RP MEGA RACER
Top-down arcade racing for the Picocomputer 6502.
Win a 5 lap race to move on to the next track.

                 Keyboard          Gamepad
Steer            Left / Right, A D d-pad / left stick
Gas              Space, Z, Up      A or R2
Brake / reverse  X, Down           B or L2
DRS boost        C, Left Shift     X
Rescue           R                 Y
Start / pause    Enter, P          Start

DRS charges while you're behind, and fast when you're
close behind the car ahead. Use it when the bar flashes.
